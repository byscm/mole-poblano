Demo: how Mole Poblano works

A step-by-step walkthrough of a real example: a hotel room filmed with a phone in two takes of about 30 s. Every result you will see was computed beforehand on a local GPU and is replayed here.

This page does not process videos. Nothing is uploaded and nothing runs in your browser. To turn your own room into 3D, run Mole Poblano on your computer with ComfyUI and Blender: setup guide →
