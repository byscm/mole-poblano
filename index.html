Mole Poblano

Photo to 3D room. Photos or a short phone video of a room become a measured floor plan and a walkable 3D scene, running entirely on your own GPU with ComfyUI and Blender.

3D viewer →Walk or fly through three example rooms, open every 3D model and see the plans. Step-by-step demo →See how a phone video becomes frames, depth, a floor plan and 3D models. Source code →Pipeline scripts, setup guide and model licences.

How it works

  1. Finds the floor, walls and ceiling, and the depth of every pixel.
  2. Cuts out, names and measures every piece of furniture, and the direction it faces.
  3. Draws a top-down plan with sizes and distances. One real measurement fixes the scale.
  4. Builds each object in 3D and places it in Blender with the same camera as the photo.
  5. Joins several photos or video frames into one room.
